# The Divine Name

*IHSVH*

יהוה — the descent that names itself

---

The Name has four letters. The whole structure descends from this.

**יהוה**

> **Number** defines, **geometry** structures, **element** qualifies, and **world** situates the one descent.

---

## I · The Name as Hierarchy

*Each letter requires the prior. The Name is the descent.*

| Letter | Register | Function |
|--------|----------|----------|
| י | Number | defines necessity |
| ה | Geometry | structures consequence |
| ו | Element | qualifies operation |
| ה | World | situates degree |

Yod alone names necessity — what must be before anything is placed. Heh structures what follows: position admitted, consequence materially built. Vav qualifies that structure as force, real not poetic. Final Heh situates the whole pattern at its level of integration. The Name is read from above; nothing below is independent of what stands above it.

## II · The Name at Each Level

*The Name speaks four languages at each level. Same reality, four namings.*

| Level | Number | Geometry | Element | World |
|-------|--------|----------|---------|-------|
| First | One | Point | Fire | Atziluth |
| Second | Two | Line | Water | Briah |
| Third | Three | Plane | Air | Yetzirah |
| Fourth | Four | Solid | Earth | Assiah |

Each row is one level of the descent named four ways. Read down a column: the register's series, productive. Read across a row: the registers correspond, no substance transmitted. Both relations hold.

## III · The Recursion

*One vertical law, two scales. One horizontal law, one scale.*

The same productive law operates twice. Down the registers: Number, Geometry, Element, World — each requires the prior. Down each register's internal series: One into Two into Three into Four; point into line into plane into solid. The Name is fractal. Each letter is itself a four-fold descent. Each level within each letter is itself a four-fold spread.

## IV · The Cumulation

*Each level contains the levels above it. The descent does not deplete the Source.*

| Row | Value |
|-----|-------|
| י | 10 |
| יה | 15 |
| יהו | 21 |
| יהוה | 26 |

10 + 15 + 21 + 26 = 72

The Name builds by accumulation. Each row is the descent so far. By the fourth, the Name is fully written.

The four rows by gematria sum to 72 — the count of the Shemhamphorasch, שם המפורש, the Explicit Name. Tradition derives it from *Exodus* 14:19-21, three verses of seventy-two Hebrew letters each, combined to yield seventy-two three-letter Names. The cumulative Tetragrammaton reaches the same number by a different path: the four-letter Name *contains* its seventy-two-fold articulation.

Manifestation is the Name written out. Atziluth, the Name unspoken; Briah, the Name voiced; Yetzirah, the Name in formation; Assiah, the Name fully written. The lower worlds are not less of God — they are more explicit articulations of the same single Name.

Non-depletion in its strongest form: the Source is present at every level, accumulated, undiminished.

## V · The Geometry: Point to Sphere

*The point that begins the descent is the point that completes it.*

Yod is the point — undivided source. Heh is the line, extension begun. Vav is the plane, surface and frame. Final Heh is the cube, the measured world: three axes through a center. The descent terminates in the cube and returns as the sphere — the center expanded equally in every direction. The point fulfilled.

*The Point Fulfilled*, in its full development, traces this geometric register from level one to its return.

## VI · The Wall

*Vertical constitutes. Horizontal corresponds. The two must not be confused.*

The productive relation transmits substance: point into line carries position into extension. The typological relation transmits none: Fire does not become Water, One does not become Point. To read horizontal correspondence as vertical constitution collapses the four registers into one. This is the single error the descent cannot tolerate.

## VII · The Disposition

God stands above what He sustains. The order cannot be proved from inside the order. Faith is the only way to navigate the mystery.
